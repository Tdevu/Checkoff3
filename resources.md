# Resources

## XOR encryption and the one-time pad
- [XOR cipher (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XOR_cipher): the identities behind XOR encryption and why a repeating key is weak
- [One-time pad (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-time_pad): when XOR encryption is unbreakable
- [Perfect Secrecy, CMSC 28400 notes by David Cash (UChicago)](https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~davidcash/284-autumn-21/04-perfect-secrecy.pdf): a proof that the one-time pad is perfectly secret (Shannon, 1949)

## Real systems broken by key reuse
- [Venona project (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venona_project): duplicate Soviet one-time pad pages let US cryptanalysts decrypt messages
- [Security of the WEP algorithm (Borisov, Goldberg, Wagner)](https://www.digiater.nl/openvms/decus/vmslt01a/net/wep-faq.html): how WEP Wi-Fi reused XOR keystreams
- [Intercepting Mobile Communications: The Insecurity of 802.11 (paper)](https://courses.cs.washington.edu/courses/csep561/08au/papers/borisov-mobicom01.pdf)
- [KRACK: Key Reinstallation Attacks (Vanhoef)](https://www.krackattacks.com): forcing nonce reuse in WPA2

## COMP 211 course material
